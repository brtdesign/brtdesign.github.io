Xeno Level Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Xeno Level collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Last updated: 5 May 2026

Overview

Xeno Level is a mobile app developed by BRT Design. The app does not require you to create an account and does not ask for personal information such as your name, email address, address, or phone number.

1. Information We Collect

The app may collect limited usage and diagnostic information through Firebase Analytics. This may include:

  • App opens
  • Screens or features used
  • Device type
  • Operating system version
  • Approximate app performance and usage events
  • App instance identifiers used by Firebase Analytics

This information is used in an aggregated way to understand how the app is used and to improve the app.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Xeno Level does not collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your contacts
  • Your photos or files
  • Your precise location
  • Payment information
  • Account login details

3. How We Use Information

We use analytics information to:

  • Understand how people use the app
  • Improve app reliability
  • Identify which features are useful
  • Help diagnose problems
  • Improve future versions of the app

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Third-Party Services

Xeno Level uses Firebase Analytics, a service provided by Google, to collect app usage analytics.

Firebase may collect and process certain device and usage information according to Google's privacy and security practices. You can learn more at Firebase Privacy and Security.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell user data.

Analytics data may be processed by Google Firebase as a service provider for the purposes described above. We may also disclose information if required to do so by law or to protect our legal rights.

6. Data Retention

Analytics data is retained according to Firebase and Google Analytics retention settings.

We only keep analytics information for as long as needed to understand app usage, improve the app, or meet legal requirements.

7. Children's Privacy

Xeno Level is not directed at children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information collected through the app. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Your Choices

You can stop further analytics collection by uninstalling the app.

You may also be able to limit app analytics or advertising identifiers through your Android device settings.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above.

11. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, contact BRT Design at ben@brtdesign.co.uk.

Policy Summary

Xeno Level does not require accounts or collect direct personal details. It may use Firebase Analytics for aggregated usage and diagnostic information.
